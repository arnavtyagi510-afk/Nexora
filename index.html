                                Student profile

                                  Add optional links, repository URLs, portfolio references, or project details. These remain student-submitted evidence until a reviewer marks a skill Verified or Rejected / More Evidence Needed.

                                    • Self-Reported: A skill is listed without submitted evidence, and it is not treated as independently verified.
                                    • Evidence Submitted: The student added a relevant URL, repository, project link, or description, but this is still not automatically verified.
                                    • Under Review: A reviewer has opened the submission for inspection.
                                    • Verified: A reviewer explicitly marked the skill as verified after local review.
                                    • Rejected / More Evidence Needed: The current evidence was not enough or was not accepted; additional details are needed.

                                    Projects and links are student-provided and not automatically verified. Skill labels remain Self-declared or Project-linked; completing Project Defense never marks a skill independently verified.

                                              Company information · local demo

                                              Company Profile & Trust Center

                                              Not Submitted

                                              Company profile and verification requests are stored only in this browser. There is no authorized reviewer or verification backend here, so a submitted request stays Pending Review and cannot become Verified in this demo. A website, email domain, or document by itself does not prove legitimacy.

                                              What verification statuses mean
                                              • Not Submitted: no request has been made; company information is unreviewed.
                                              • Pending Review: submitted for review; no approval has been granted.
                                              • Verified: approved through a real, documented verification process. This demo has no such process and cannot grant this status.
                                              • Rejected: a reviewer declined a request and supplied feedback. This demo cannot make reviewer decisions.
